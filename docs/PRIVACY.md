# Deltarium privacy policy

Deltarium runs entirely on your device. It makes no network calls, shows no
ads, has no accounts or sign-in, and contains no analytics or third-party
code. It collects no data of any kind, about you or about anyone.

Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing more for this policy to
describe: no data is gathered, stored, shared, or sold, and nothing ever
leaves your phone.

If you have a question about this policy, open an issue at
<https://github.com/evanleeturner/deltarium-iphone/issues> or write to
evanlee.turner@gmail.com.

Effective 2026-07-26. If a future version of the app changes any of the
above, this page will change with it and the effective date will move.
